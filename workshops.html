Learn from our growers

Workshops at the farm

Hands-on classes led by experienced growers at our Elora farm. Spend a day learning to graft and prune your own fruit trees — skills you’ll put to work in your orchard for years to come.

01 Sold out
April 49:30am – 12:00pm

Grafting Workshop

$175 + tax

Learn the craft of grafting from our growers. We’ll cover the history and techniques behind propagating fruit trees, then you’ll graft your own to take home and plant.

What you’ll learn
  • The history and different techniques of grafting
  • How to select and store dormant scion wood
  • Hands-on cleft grafting — joining scions onto rootstock
  • Chip-bud and top-graft demonstrations
What’s included
  • 5 apple scions and 5 rootstocks to graft
  • A grafting knife to take home
  • 5 grafted fruit trees to plant
  • Refreshments throughout the morning

Held indoors and outdoors — please dress for the weather. Limited to 20 seats.

02 Sold out
April 42:00pm – 5:00pm

Pruning Workshop

$140 + tax

A practical afternoon on pruning fruit trees — one hour of indoor instruction followed by two hours of hands-on work in the Whiffletree orchard.

What you’ll learn
  • Pruning techniques and strategies
  • How pruning affects tree health and productivity
  • Ideal branch structure
  • Pruning trees of various ages and species
Led by Matt Soltys — The Urban Orchardist
Matt studied biology at the University of Guelph and managed a community orchard before founding his own orchard consulting and design practice.

Bring your own clean pruning tools — sanitizing product provided. Limited to 20 seats.
