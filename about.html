Our story

About Whiffletree

Established in 2012

Since 2012, Whiffletree Farm & Nursery has been proudly owned and operated by brothers Lawrence and Lowell Martin, along with our dedicated families.

Nestled just north of Kitchener/Waterloo, Ontario, near the quaint town of Elmira, our family-run business is rooted in a deep passion for sustainable farming and nurturing the environment.

Meet the Martins

Lawrence and Lowell Martin, along with their families, bring a wealth of knowledge and experience to Whiffletree Farm & Nursery. Their commitment to excellence and passion for what they do is evident in every aspect of the business.

From hand-selecting the best plants to providing personalized advice, the Martins are dedicated to helping you achieve your gardening goals.

Our mission

Our mission is simple: to inspire and support our community in their gardening and farming endeavors. We strive to offer a diverse selection of plants and trees that are not only beautiful but also well-suited to our local climate and conditions.

Whether you’re a seasoned gardener or just starting out, we’re here to help you every step of the way.

Our values

The principles that guide how we farm, support our community, and grow together as a family.

Sustainable farming
We are dedicated to sustainable farming practices that respect the land and promote a healthy ecosystem.
Community
We believe in the importance of community and the power of working together to create a greener, more beautiful world.
Family
As a family-owned business, we understand the value of family and strive to create an environment where families can learn, grow, and thrive together.
