Payment

Card is charged now. e-Transfer and credit-union bill payment are confirmed once funds arrive — your order is held as “awaiting payment” until then.

Place the order first — we’ll email your order number and full Interac e-Transfer instructions right away. To send it:

  1. Send the e-Transfer to pay@whiffletree.ca.
  2. In the message/memo field, put your order number (e.g. WT-10432) so we can match your payment to your order.

Your order is reserved as awaiting payment until the transfer arrives. Payments without an order number take longer to confirm.

Pay from your bank or credit union’s online bill payment. To set it up:

  1. Add Whiffletree Farm & Nursery as a payee.
  2. Use your order number (e.g. WT-10432) as the account number so we can match your payment to your order.
