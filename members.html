Featured grower · July 2026

Sarah Mercer

Elmvale, Ontario · Zone 4 · Growing since 2019

“I chose varieties for our short season and never looked back.”

Sarah started with three bare-root apples along the back fence in 2019. Six seasons later, her half-acre in Elmvale is a working little orchard — and every tree came from Whiffletree.

The Honeycrisp is finally fruiting, the cherries draw every robin in the county, and the kids can’t keep their hands off the raspberries.
